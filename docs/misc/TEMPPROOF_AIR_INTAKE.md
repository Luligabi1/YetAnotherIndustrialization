# Adding biomes for Tempproof Air Intakes 

**Tempproof Air Intakes** are used to generate otherworld liquid airs. All biomes are supported as long as they fit in the criteria below:

- **Scorching Liquid Air**
    - Must be in `#c:is_hot` and not on `#c:is_hot/overworld`
- **Gelid Liquid Air**
    - Must be in `#c:is_cold` and not on `#c:is_cold/overworld`

If you wish to support a biome without changing which tags they belong to, you can also add them directly to the respective tags:

- `#yet_another_industrialization:gelid_liquid_air_biomes`
- `#yet_another_industrialization:scorching_liquid_air_biomes`
