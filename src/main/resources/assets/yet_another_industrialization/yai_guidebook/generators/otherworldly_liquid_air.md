# Otherwordly Liquid Airs
###### *'cause you're hot, then you're cold*

Air composition is not the same everywhere. By harnessing another dimensions', you are capable of generating energy and extracting resources!

Otherwordly places's inhospitable climate makes regular Air Intakes incapable of working properly. Use **Tempproof Air Intakes** instead:

<Recipe id="yet_another_industrialization:craft/tempproof_air_intake" />

Scalding hot dimensions, like the **Nether**, will have **Scorching Liquid Air** and will require small amounts of **Cryofluid** to extract.

Frigid cold dimensions, like **The End**, will have **Gelid Liquid Air** and will require small amounts of **Lava** to extract.

## Producing energy

Combine **Scorching** and **Gelid** Liquid Airs in a **Heat Exchanger** to create a ton of **Steam**!

## Centrifuging

Centrifuging **Scorching Liquid Air** yields tiny amounts of **Sulphur**, **Gold** and **Lava**.

Centrifuging **Gelid Liquid Air** yields tiny amounts of **Beryllium**, **Tungsten** and **Argon**.
